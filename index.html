KissDisplay: signage that just loops

Put your photos and videos on any screen and they loop seamlessly with nothing else on screen. Schedule playlists through the day, add specials on the hour, your logo and a message ticker, and run every screen in your venue from one phone, tablet or laptop.

Getting started

  • Screens: open KissDisplay on each screen (Apple TV, Android TV or projector, or an iPad in screen mode). It shows a QR code and a 4-digit code.
  • Host: in KissDisplay on your iPhone or iPad, open Screens › Add Screen and scan the code. On a laptop, open the web host and enter the screen's address and code.
  • Content: build playlists, then choose what each screen plays and tap Send Content. Only new photos and videos are sent.
  • Same network: the host and the screens need to be on the same Wi-Fi or wired network.
  • Leaving the display: on Android TV and Apple TV press Back/Menu; on iPhone and iPad hold the middle of the screen for 4 seconds. If you set an exit code, it's needed here.
