Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

KissDisplay is made by Andrew Stokes (datadrvn). This policy covers KissDisplay for iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Android, and the KissDisplay web host.

What the apps store

The photos, videos, playlists, schedules, logo and messages you add are stored only on your own devices. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. KissDisplay has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not collect your name, contacts, location or any identifiers.

Your local network

A host (KissDisplay on an iPhone or iPad, or the web host in your browser) sends your content directly to your venue's screens over your own local network. Nothing passes through our servers. Screens show a pairing code, and once added to a venue they only accept that venue's host.

Camera and photos

On iPhone and iPad, the camera is used only to scan the QR code a screen shows, and the image is not stored. Photos and videos you add are chosen with the system picker; the app only receives the items you choose, and stores copies on your device.

Web host

The web host keeps your library in your browser's own storage on your computer. It connects only to the screens you add.
